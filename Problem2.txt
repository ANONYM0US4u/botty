One subtle issue I spotted

First real training run: python -m engine.agents.ppo trains with 2019–2023 NSE data. �

But the data specification says yfinance is providing 2+ years of minute bars. �
2026-08-19-trading-bot-design.md
Those aren't automatically compatible.
If you're building this in August 2026 and only have two-ish years of minute history, you cannot silently promise a 2019–2023 minute dataset.
Make the data availability check an explicit pre-training gate.
Something like:
DATASET VALIDATION
✓ requested date range exists
✓ required timeframe exists
✓ no duplicate timestamps
✓ no future timestamps
✓ no unexpected gaps
✓ OHLC relationships valid
✓ timezone normalized
✓ corporate-action policy applied
Then training is allowed.
Otherwise:
DATASET INVALID
→ TRAINING BLOCKED
That is much safer than discovering it halfway through training.
